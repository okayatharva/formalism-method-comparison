# By Operation — 10 New Papers

This is the one I care about most.

- Learned attention (learn who/what to trust more): Transformer, ABMIL, Hopfield
- Aggregating from neighbors on a graph: DGCNN, GIN
- Just taking the max value across everything: PointNet
- Comparing every pair and combining the results: Relation Networks
- Mixing information along two fixed directions of a table: MLP-Mixer
- Encoding everything, averaging it, then predicting from that: CNP
- Weighing neighbors based on distance: SchNet

The interesting part: Transformer, ABMIL, and Hopfield all land in the same
group here, even though one is about translating sentences, one is about
reading medical scans, and one is about modeling memory. None of these
fields have anything to do with each other, but they're doing the exact
same thing underneath.
